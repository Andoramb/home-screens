## New in this RC

- The docs and README cover everything new in 1.13.
- Underlying libraries are updated, including security fixes in the web framework and an image library that could hang on a malformed picture.
- The Discord link in Settings, the README and the docs points at a fresh invite.

## New

- School Timetable module. A week card per child with today lit, start and end times and what to pack, plus a Day view that puts every child on a shared clock. Schools hold their bell times, breaks, A and B weeks and exceptions, weeks import from a Google Sheet or CSV, and holidays come from OpenHolidays where published.
- Shared to-do lists. The phone, the wall and the editor read and write the same list. The phone gains a Lists tab, and the To-Do module offers list, focus, progress, board and compact views with due days, repeats and assignment.
- Family roster and groups. Household members live in one roster shared by chores, calendars, to-do lists and timetables, and removing a person cleans up every reference. A named group (Kids, Grown-ups) works anywhere people are picked.
- Group chores, bonus chores and "not today". A group chore reaches everyone in the group, including people who join later. Bonus chores are up for grabs or open to everyone and earn tickets without counting toward daily chores or streaks. Grown-ups can skip a chore for one person for a day without breaking their streak.
- Calendar name tags, a people filter and a rolling weeks view. Both calendar modules can show the owner's initials in place of the calendar dot, show only the people or groups you choose, and show one to eight weeks from today.
- Calendar day rules can match specific dates (a day of every month, a fixed date each year, the nth weekday, a whole month) and paint a built-in or uploaded picture behind the day.
- Pictures and videos page. The whole library with sizes and folders, bulk upload, move and replace in place, an In use badge, and a report of files a screen expects but the library no longer has.
- Photo library on the phone. Browse by folder, open any photo full screen, show it on the wall, move or delete it, manage folders, and add pictures from Google Photos or an iCloud link. A Slideshow card steps, pauses and plays the wall's slideshow.
- Your own pictures as icons. Upload a PNG, JPG, WebP or GIF from any icon picker, crop it square, and use it for meals, chores, rewards, people, routines and screens.
- Rewards store and reward history on the chore charts: a list, tiles or a price list that shows who can afford each reward, and what the family has redeemed day by day, as 30-day totals or in the spotlight.
- Update channels, automatic updates and safer updates. Choose Normal, Early access, Beta or Test builds, let the device install at a chosen time of day, and rely on a release that can require a version first, hold back a step the saved settings cannot survive, and roll itself back if it never starts.
- Background shade. Each screen can darken its background picture with an even shade, a fade or darker edges so text stays readable.
- Clock backdrop on the photo viewer, from none to a solid band, with a color picker and digits that pick dark or light to match.
- Screen dots can be hidden from Settings > Screen, for every display or just one.
- Clock check on the Location page sets the device's clock to the household's time zone with one button.
- Panel power-off while the display sleeps, an opt-in toggle under Sleep & dimming.
- Touch alignment on rotated touchscreens follows the rotation, survives updates, and can take your own six numbers.
- Docker. Build and run the hub as a Docker image, with an example compose file.
- Sign in to Google Calendar and Google Photos with Home Screens' own Google app, hidden until Google approves it.
- Home Assistant and other tools can show or hide one module with the display key.
- Restart to finish the update is offered on the System page, the phone and the update window whenever a kiosk restart is needed.

## Improved

- Every screen follows the household's time zone, never the device or browser clock, and looking up a town fills in its time zone. A new install's 12 or 24 hour clock follows the chosen language.
- A much lighter wall on a Pi: one heartbeat instead of five polls, fetches of only what changed, cached calendar feeds, and clock, calendar and meal modules that redraw only what moves. A Pi 4 clock and chore wall drops from two thirds of a core to under a third.
- Wall photos load at the size they are shown, stay loaded across screen changes, are fetched ahead and fade in once ready, and slideshows pick up where they left off. New picture, video, slideshow, web embed and rain map cards start without a card blur.
- The Pi's rotation and size follow the main display's settings without a reboot, survive an update, and hold when a monitor drops off HDMI in standby.
- Updates work on installs that are not a Raspberry Pi, say in plain language when they cannot start, download less, and hide the offer while installing. Display-only Pis check every hour.
- Recovery from a broken setup: a Try again screen with backup tools when the saved setup cannot load, pages that recover from an error by themselves, a half-written module that draws stand-ins, and failed saves and loads that speak up on the phone and in the editor.
- Editing at the same time from two phones no longer loses a routine change.
- Chore chart and chore form: one row per chore with a dot per person (tap to tick, hold to un-tick), people without an icon show their initial, Schedule is always offered, every schedule row has a remove button, chores show their ticket value, and weekly chores ask for at least one day.
- Calendar: day rules can be named and open one at a time, event backgrounds can be turned off on the daily and agenda views, and Wrap titles is offered on the rolling weeks view.
- Daily weather forecasts can hide the weekday labels, the larger first day and the Forecast heading, and the editor's weather preview asks only the services you use.
- Meals can be typed straight into a day, and the grocery list guesses the aisle in every language.
- Settings search finds settings by their options and help text and says which page each is on, with clearer wording across Settings in every language.
- Editor and Style panel: background and border colors have a strength slider, the see-through slider is its own Color strength row, each display's preview and swatches use its own theme, and modules dragged past the edge land at the edge.
- Phone: the Back gesture closes the open sheet and asks first about unsaved changes, sheets take keyboard focus and close on Escape, and tabs reload when the network is back.
- Picture library: SVG and the other JPEG spellings are accepted, only a slideshow's last playable picture and files a screen uses are protected from deletion, and deleting moves onto the Pictures and videos page.
- Small improvements: a long press on the kiosk touchscreen no longer opens the browser's menu, the display holds the current screen for eight seconds after a tap, un-ticking a finished chore on the wall takes a press and hold, an empty screen stays out of the rotation, Suggest and plugin uninstall ask first, holiday countries and new screen names follow the household's language, the getting-started checklist has a household step, and the Rain Map timestamp uses the module's font.
- The website's module options, configuration types and endpoint list are generated from the code, the API reference is rewritten, and the docs and README cover everything new in 1.13.
- Underlying libraries are updated, including security fixes in the web framework, the test runner and an image library that could hang on a malformed picture.
- A batch of wording and layout tidy-ups across the editor, the phone and the wall.

## Fixed

- Two phones, or a phone and the editor, changing chores, rewards, meals or routines at the same time can no longer silently drop each other's changes.
- Running the installer over an existing install destroyed every uploaded picture and iCloud import. User files now survive a re-install.
- The editor password could go unchecked on some installs because the password file was read from the wrong place.
- Checking off a chore and crediting its points are saved together, so losing power in between cannot record one without the other.
- A display held at a standing brightness ignored every tap and flick.
- On a Pi with more than one video output, the kiosk drives the output that is actually switched on.
- A display could show old data when a request that started before a change finished after it.
- Calendar feeds whose events carry a time but no time zone resolve in the display's time zone, so a 4 pm event no longer shows at 6 pm on a UTC hub.
- Chore and reward fixes: weekly rotations hand over on Monday instead of Friday, two quick taps on the same chore no longer undo each other, un-ticking a chore whose points were spent says who is affected, adding a member before the chore manager had loaded discarded the name, removing a person switches off a reward that was only theirs, both charts show a loading message on a fresh Pi, and the kids' page works with a password set.
- Calendar fixes: day-rule pictures paint under events and load behind a password, switching a rule to weekdays clears the month, a just-checked feed keeps its health badge, Google Calendar's newer colors show, and Todoist tasks with a time show at the right time.
- Weather and sports fixes: Met Office and Open-Meteo forecasts start on the right day, switching to a source with a saved key no longer flashes a missing-key card, and Sports Standings with no grouping chosen labelled one grouping as another.
- Photo and iCloud fixes: videos shown from the phone fill the wall, sideways photos report their upright size, folder create and delete report the right thing, newer Shared Album links work, and an unreachable iCloud is reported as such.
- Wall freshness fixes: the first layout edit no longer empties the wall's cache, a Google Calendar sign-in shows within seconds, a refused display key reloads to pick up a new one, and the phone and kids' page pick up chores and rewards another phone changed.
- Date fixes: new countdown and holiday dates no longer start a day off when picked in the evening, and a one-sided time window reads "from 6:00 PM" or "until 9:00 AM" instead of "all day".
- Layout fixes: long all-day titles stay in their day in the schedule view, the meal planner's week view fits a landscape wall, and the photo viewer's clock shows on the wall when the editor says it is on.
- Plugin fixes: installing or removing one no longer resets the panel to Browse, and two quick changes no longer leave the hub serving the previous plugin list.
- Small fixes: completed to-do items are tidied after 30 days, with idle sleep off the remote's Sleep no longer lights a powered-off screen, the phone's Back gesture closes the photo viewer, a backup from a newer version is refused with a reason, arrow keys inside an editor control no longer move the module, a space in a screen rename no longer starts a drag, grocery ticks and blank ingredient rows behave, mislabelled options in four languages are corrected, the Discord link points at a fresh invite, and dev servers no longer count as new installs in the anonymous telemetry.
