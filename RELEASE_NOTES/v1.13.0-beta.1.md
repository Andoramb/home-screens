## New in this beta

- Family groups. Name a set of people once (Kids, Grown-ups) under Family in the editor or on the phone, then use it anywhere people are picked. Removing a person takes them out of every group, and two groups cannot share a name.
- Calendar name tags and a people filter. Both calendar modules can show the owner's initials in their color in place of the calendar dot, list calendars, people or groups in the color key, and show only the people or groups you choose.
- Chores for a whole group. Give a chore to a family group and everyone in it gets the chore, including someone who joins the group later. A group can have its own row on a chore's schedule, and the Full-Screen Chore Chart draws a group chore as its members' rings inside a labelled pill. Removing a group says how many chores it has first and tidies them up in the same step.
- Screen dots setting. Hide the dots at the bottom of the display from Settings > Screen, for every display or just one. Turning them off also turns off double-tap pause and the progress line.
- Set the device's clock from the editor. When the device's time zone disagrees with the one chosen for the display, the Timezone setting says so and offers one button to fix it.
- Safer updates. A release can name a version that must be installed first, and the update check offers that step instead of jumping past it. A step back to a build that cannot read your saved settings is held back. If a new release never starts, the previous version is put back on its own and the System page explains what happened.
- The Pi's screen rotation and size follow the main display's settings. A rotation changed on a per-display page takes effect without a reboot, and an update no longer resets it to the shared values.
- Updates work on installs that are not a Raspberry Pi, including Ubuntu and installs kept in a different folder. When an update cannot start, the message says why in plain language.
- The update offer hides while an update is installing, so the editor and the phone stop suggesting a version that is already on its way.
- Schedule is always offered on the chore form, every schedule row has a remove button, and the phone's schedule rows are easier to read. A scheduled chore that goes to nobody is flagged in both chore lists.
- People without an icon show their initial on the chore chart's Today view.
- The To-Do module's tap-to-complete setting is worded the same as the Todoist module's in all seven languages.
- Two phones, or a phone and the editor, changing chores, rewards or meals at the same time can no longer silently drop each other's changes. Meal edits are re-applied on top of the newer copy, and chores and rewards show the current list and ask you to make the change again.
- A display held at a standing brightness ignored every tap and flick. It now responds to touch like a normal screen.
- On a Pi with more than one video output, the kiosk now drives the output that is actually switched on rather than the first one listed.
- Weekly and every-other-week chore rotations hand over on Monday instead of Friday.
- A display could show old data after a change when a request that started before the change finished after it.
- Sports Standings with no grouping chosen fetched one grouping and labelled it as another.
- Un-ticking a chore whose points were already spent now says who is affected and by how much on the phone.
- The Danish chore rotation labels are corrected.

## New

- School Timetable module. One week card per child on the wall, with today lit, the day's start and end times, what to pack, and a turn to next week once the school week is over. A Day view puts every child on a shared clock, with a line at the current time, a packing list, and a switch to the next school day at a time you choose. Schools own their bell times, breaks, after-school care, A and B week cycles and dated exceptions, so siblings at one school are typed in once. Weeks can be imported from a Google Sheet or a CSV file, with an optional hourly re-check that never overwrites hand edits. School and public holidays come from OpenHolidays for regions that publish them. A Dates tab holds tests, things to bring and cancelled lessons. Ships in all seven languages, each with its own subject list.
- Shared to-do lists. Items now live in one place, so the phone, the wall and the editor all read and write the same list. The family remote gains a Lists tab with drag to reorder and a share sheet. The To-Do module offers list, focus, progress, board and compact views, due days, and repeats that start fresh on schedule. Items can be assigned to people from the family roster, shown as initials on the wall and the phone.
- Family roster. Household members move out of the chore chart into one shared roster used by chores, calendars, to-do lists and timetables. Manage it under Settings > Family in the editor or Family in the phone's Settings, with or without a chore chart. Removing a person cleans up every place they were referenced, and restoring a backup refuses to leave assignments pointing at people the backup does not have.
- Family groups. Name a set of people once (Kids, Grown-ups) under Family in the editor or on the phone, then use it anywhere people are picked. Removing a person takes them out of every group, and two groups cannot share a name.
- Calendar name tags and a people filter. Both calendar modules can show the owner's initials in their color in place of the calendar dot, list calendars, people or groups in the color key, and show only the people or groups you choose.
- Chores for a whole group. Give a chore to a family group and everyone in it gets the chore, including someone who joins the group later. A group can have its own row on a chore's schedule, and the Full-Screen Chore Chart draws a group chore as its members' rings inside a labelled pill. Removing a group says how many chores it has first and tidies them up in the same step.
- Pictures and videos page. A new settings page lists every picture and video in your library with its size and dimensions, grouped by folder and filtered by kind or by unused only. Upload many files at once into a folder, create, rename and delete folders, move files between them, and replace a file in place while every screen keeps pointing at it. Files still in use show an In use badge that says where, and cannot be deleted from anywhere. A viewer shows each file's facts with previous and next, and a missing-file report flags anything a screen expects but the library no longer has.
- Calendar day rules can now match specific dates. Pick a day of every month, a fixed date each year, the last day of the month, the nth weekday of a month, or a whole month, alongside the existing weekday and date-range options.
- Calendar day rules can paint a picture behind a day. Choose from ten built-in pictures (birthday, Christmas, Halloween, New Year, Valentine's, July 4th and more) or upload your own, with a dimming slider so event text stays readable, and size and position controls for how the picture sits in the cell.
- Rolling weeks view for both the compact and full-screen calendars. Shows one to eight weeks starting from today, so the grid never opens on a week that has already passed.
- Screen dots setting. Hide the dots at the bottom of the display from Settings > Screen, for every display or just one. Turning them off also turns off double-tap pause and the progress line.
- Set the device's clock from the editor. When the device's time zone disagrees with the one chosen for the display, the Timezone setting says so and offers one button to fix it.
- Update channels. Choose Normal, Early access or Beta under updates, or Test builds with advanced options on, and see the build label next to the version. A lower-numbered offer is worded as a switch, not an update, and the last stable config is pinned in Backups & data before a prerelease build replaces it.
- Safer updates. A release can name a version that must be installed first, and the update check offers that step instead of jumping past it. A step back to a build that cannot read your saved settings is held back. If a new release never starts, the previous version is put back on its own and the System page explains what happened.
- Panel power-off while the display sleeps. An opt-in toggle under Sleep & dimming cuts the screen's power whenever the display is asleep, from any sleep source, and the per-display Overview page shows whether power control is working.

## Improved

- The Pi's screen rotation and size follow the main display's settings. A rotation changed on a per-display page takes effect without a reboot, and an update no longer resets it to the shared values.
- Updates work on installs that are not a Raspberry Pi, including Ubuntu and installs kept in a different folder. When an update cannot start, the message says why in plain language.
- The update offer hides while an update is installing, so the editor and the phone stop suggesting a version that is already on its way.
- Schedule is always offered on the chore form, every schedule row has a remove button, and the phone's schedule rows are easier to read. A scheduled chore that goes to nobody is flagged in both chore lists.
- People without an icon show their initial on the chore chart's Today view.
- The To-Do module's tap-to-complete setting is worded the same as the Todoist module's in all seven languages.
- Calendar day rules can be named (Halloween, Trash day) and now show as a collapsed list that opens one rule at a time, so a long set of rules no longer reads as a wall of forms. Duplicate names are refused.
- A long press on the kiosk touchscreen no longer opens Chromium's browser menu.
- The picture library accepts SVG files, plus the jfif, pjpeg and pjp spellings of JPEG, and lists them alongside your other uploads. SVGs without a declared size are refused because they would stretch instead of scale.
- Deleting library files moves out of the background and day-art pickers and onto the Pictures and videos page, where files still in use are protected. Removing a day-rule picture asks first and reports a failure instead of staying silent.
- The Rain Map timestamp uses the module's Style font like the rest of the module and keeps a steady width as it ticks.
- The Full-Screen Calendar's Wrap titles toggle is now offered on the rolling weeks view too.
- Update downloads are smaller, and only the newest nightly test build is kept on the releases page so real releases stay easy to find.
- The website's module options, configuration types and endpoint list are generated from the code, so the reference pages can no longer drift from what the app actually accepts. The API reference is rewritten around display control, chores, meals, timers and backups.
- Underlying libraries are updated, including security fixes in the web framework, the test runner and the image processing libraries.

## Fixed

- Two phones, or a phone and the editor, changing chores, rewards or meals at the same time can no longer silently drop each other's changes. Meal edits are re-applied on top of the newer copy, and chores and rewards show the current list and ask you to make the change again.
- A display held at a standing brightness ignored every tap and flick. It now responds to touch like a normal screen.
- On a Pi with more than one video output, the kiosk now drives the output that is actually switched on rather than the first one listed.
- Weekly and every-other-week chore rotations hand over on Monday instead of Friday.
- A display could show old data after a change when a request that started before the change finished after it.
- Sports Standings with no grouping chosen fetched one grouping and labelled it as another.
- Un-ticking a chore whose points were already spent now says who is affected and by how much on the phone.
- The Danish chore rotation labels are corrected.
- Calendar feeds whose events carry a time but no time zone now resolve in the display's time zone instead of the hub's, so a 4 pm event no longer shows at 6 pm when the hub runs on UTC.
- Running the installer over an existing install destroyed every uploaded picture and iCloud import. User files are now preserved across a re-install, and a failure partway through no longer strands your data in a temporary folder.
- The editor password could go unchecked on some installs because the password file was read from the wrong place, letting saves through without signing in. The gate now reads the same file the login writes.
- Day-rule pictures paint under the day's number and events rather than over them, load correctly on a wall behind a password, and no longer wipe out a rule's colour when the base background changes. Existing rules keep their fill look in landscape cells instead of showing bands.
- Checking off a chore and crediting its reward points are saved together, so losing power in between can no longer record the chore without the points.
- Completed to-do items are tidied after 30 days, so a list that never repeats cannot fill up and refuse new items.
- Adding a household member in the chore manager before its data had loaded discarded the new name.
- Installing or removing a plugin no longer resets the plugin panel back to its Browse tab, and a just-checked calendar feed keeps its health badge when an older status check lands late.
- Two quick back-to-back plugin changes could leave the hub serving the previous plugin list.
- Switching a day rule from a date pattern to the weekday pattern kept the chosen month, and a picture the rule still holds shows as selected even after it was moved or renamed in Pictures and videos.
