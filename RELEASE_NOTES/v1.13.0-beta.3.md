## New in this beta

- Docker. Build and run the hub as a Docker image from the source tree, with an example compose file.
- Photo library on the phone. The Photos tab shows the whole library: browse by folder, open any photo full screen, show it on the wall, move or delete it, select several at once, manage folders, and add pictures from Google Photos or an iCloud link. Photos are shrunk before sending and videos upload with progress. A Slideshow card on the Control tab steps, pauses and plays the wall's slideshow.
- Clock backdrop on the photo viewer. A slider from no backdrop to a solid band behind the clock, a color picker for it, and digits that pick dark or light to match.
- Sign in to Google Calendar and Google Photos with Home Screens' own Google app, so a household no longer needs its own Google Cloud project. Hidden until Google approves the app.
- Home Assistant and other tools can show or hide one module with the display key.
- Restart to finish the update. When an update needs a kiosk restart, the System page, the phone and the update window say so and offer it.
- A much lighter wall on a Pi. Five polls became one heartbeat, the wall fetches only what changed, calendar feeds are cached, and the moving parts of the clock, calendar and meal modules redraw only when they move. A Pi 4 clock and chore wall drops from two thirds of a core to under a third and stops dropping frames.
- Wall photos load at the size they are shown, stay loaded across screen changes, are fetched before the next screen arrives, and fade in only once ready. Slideshows pick up where they left off, and rotating backgrounds no longer flash the screen's own picture first.
- New picture, video, slideshow, web embed and rain map cards start without a card blur, which the Pi cannot draw cheaply. Saved cards keep theirs.
- The screen keeps its rotation when a monitor drops off HDMI in standby and comes back.
- Each display's editor preview, theme swatches and photo viewer defaults use that display's own theme.
- Photo library housekeeping: only a slideshow's last playable picture and files a screen uses are protected from deletion, the badge says which, and folders, uploads and the open-folder memory behave more predictably.
- The Style panel's see-through slider is its own "Color strength" row, and long color labels hyphenate in German, Dutch and Danish.
- Editing at the same time from two phones no longer loses a routine change; the phone re-applies its edit on top of the newer copy.
- Recovery from a broken setup. The editor and Settings show a Try again screen with backup tools when the saved setup cannot load, every page recovers from an error by itself, and a half-written module draws with stand-ins instead of taking down the wall.
- Failed saves and loads speak up on the phone and in the editor instead of failing silently, and the phone's tabs reload when the network is back.
- Suggest and plugin uninstall ask first.
- Editor canvas fixes: modules dragged past the edge land at the edge, full-screen modules stay full-screen, screens copied to another display shape are fitted, and the position and size boxes accept a retyped number.
- Phone sheets take keyboard focus, keep Tab inside and close on Escape.
- Holiday countries and new screen names follow the household's language.
- A batch of wording and layout tidy-ups across the editor, the phone and the wall.
- Wall freshness fixes: the first layout edit no longer empties the wall's cache, a Google Calendar sign-in shows within seconds, a refused display key reloads to pick up a new one, and the phone and kids' page pick up chores and rewards another phone changed.
- The kids' page works with a password set.
- Photo fixes: videos shown from the phone fill the wall, Show on the wall sends a fitted copy, sideways photos report their upright size, and folder create and delete report the right thing.
- Chore and reward fixes: removing a person switches off a reward that was only theirs, and grocery ticks, blank ingredient rows and the "Other" aisle behave.
- Layout fixes: long all-day titles stay in their day in the calendar's schedule view, the meal planner's week view fits a landscape wall, and the photo viewer's clock shows on the wall when the editor says it is on.
- Sleep fix: with idle sleep off, the remote's Sleep no longer turns back into dimmed and lights a powered-off screen.
- Small fixes: the phone's Back gesture closes the photo viewer, a backup from a newer version is refused with a reason, arrow keys inside an editor control no longer move the module, typing a space in a screen rename no longer starts a drag, and two mislabelled options in German, French, Brazilian Portuguese and Danish are corrected.
- Dev servers and test sandboxes no longer count as new installs in the anonymous telemetry.

## New

- School Timetable module. One week card per child with today lit, start and end times, what to pack, and a turn to next week once the school week is over. A Day view puts every child on a shared clock with a line at the current time. Schools own their bell times, breaks, A and B weeks and dated exceptions, so siblings at one school are typed in once. Weeks import from a Google Sheet or CSV, holidays come from OpenHolidays where published, and a Dates tab holds tests, things to bring and cancelled lessons. Ships in all seven languages.
- Shared to-do lists. Items live in one place, so the phone, the wall and the editor read and write the same list. The phone gains a Lists tab with drag to reorder and a share sheet. The To-Do module offers list, focus, progress, board and compact views, due days, repeats, and assignment to people from the family roster.
- Family roster. Household members move into one shared roster used by chores, calendars, to-do lists and timetables, managed under Settings > Family or on the phone. Removing a person cleans up every reference, and restoring a backup refuses to leave assignments pointing at people it does not have.
- Family groups. Name a set of people once (Kids, Grown-ups) and use it anywhere people are picked.
- Calendar name tags and a people filter. Both calendar modules can show the owner's initials in their color instead of the calendar dot, list people or groups in the color key, and show only the people or groups you choose.
- Chores for a whole group. Give a chore to a family group and everyone in it gets it, including people who join later. A group can have its own schedule row, and the Full-Screen Chore Chart draws a group chore as its members' rings in a labelled pill.
- Pictures and videos page. Every picture and video in the library with its size, grouped by folder and filterable. Upload many at once, manage folders, move and replace files in place, and see an In use badge that says where a file is used. A missing-file report flags anything a screen expects but the library no longer has.
- Calendar day rules can match specific dates (a day of every month, a fixed date each year, the last day of the month, the nth weekday, a whole month) and can paint a picture behind a day, from ten built-in pictures or your own, with dimming, size and position controls.
- Rolling weeks view for both calendars. Shows one to eight weeks starting from today.
- Screen dots setting. Hide the dots at the bottom of the display from Settings > Screen, for every display or just one.
- Set the device's clock from the editor. When the device's time zone disagrees with the household's, Clock check on the Location page offers one button to fix it.
- Update channels. Choose Normal, Early access or Beta under updates, or Test builds with advanced options on. A lower-numbered offer is worded as a switch, and the last stable config is pinned in Backups & data before a prerelease build replaces it.
- Safer updates. A release can name a version that must be installed first, a step back to a build that cannot read your saved settings is held back, and if a new release never starts the previous version is put back on its own.
- Panel power-off while the display sleeps. An opt-in toggle under Sleep & dimming cuts the screen's power whenever the display is asleep.
- Bonus chores. A chore can be up for grabs or open to everyone, and comes back daily, weekly or when put back. Kids grab, let go and finish bonus chores on the phone and the wall. Bonus chores earn tickets but never count toward daily chores, stars or streaks.
- Not today. Grown-ups can mark a chore "not today" for one person without breaking their streak.
- Your own pictures as icons. Upload a PNG, JPG, WebP or GIF from any icon picker and use it for meals, chores, rewards, people, routines and screens, with a review step and square crop. A Your icons page shows where each picture is used.
- Automatic updates. Turn them on in Settings > System & updates and pick a time of day. The device checks once a day and only installs a newer version that is ready to go.
- Background shade. Each screen can darken its background picture with an even shade, a fade, darker edges or both, so text stays readable over bright photos.
- Rewards store on the chore chart card, as a list, tiles or a price list that shows who can afford each reward. It asks before spending tickets.
- Reward history. Both chore charts can show what the family has redeemed: day by day, 30-day totals, or the newest reward in the spotlight.
- Touch alignment. Taps land in the right place on a rotated touchscreen and the alignment survives updates. A Touch alignment setting can follow the rotation, leave touch alone, or take your own six numbers.
- Docker. Build and run the hub as a Docker image from the source tree, with an example compose file.
- Photo library on the phone. The Photos tab shows the whole library: browse by folder, open any photo full screen, show it on the wall, move or delete it, select several at once, manage folders, and add pictures from Google Photos or an iCloud link. Photos are shrunk before sending and videos upload with progress. A Slideshow card on the Control tab steps, pauses and plays the wall's slideshow.
- Clock backdrop on the photo viewer. A slider from no backdrop to a solid band behind the clock, a color picker for it, and digits that pick dark or light to match.
- Sign in to Google Calendar and Google Photos with Home Screens' own Google app, so a household no longer needs its own Google Cloud project. Hidden until Google approves the app.
- Home Assistant and other tools can show or hide one module with the display key.
- Restart to finish the update. When an update needs a kiosk restart, the System page, the phone and the update window say so and offer it.

## Improved

- The Pi's screen rotation and size follow the main display's settings, take effect without a reboot, and survive an update.
- Updates work on installs that are not a Raspberry Pi, including Ubuntu and installs in a different folder, and say why in plain language when they cannot start. The update offer hides while an update is installing.
- Chore form: Schedule is always offered, every schedule row has a remove button, a scheduled chore that goes to nobody is flagged, chores show their ticket value, and weekly chores ask for at least one day.
- People without an icon show their initial on the chore chart's Today view.
- Calendar day rules can be named and show as a collapsed list that opens one rule at a time.
- A long press on the kiosk touchscreen no longer opens Chromium's browser menu.
- The picture library accepts SVG files and the other JPEG spellings. Deleting library files moves onto the Pictures and videos page, where files in use are protected.
- The Rain Map timestamp uses the module's Style font and keeps a steady width.
- The Full-Screen Calendar's Wrap titles toggle is offered on the rolling weeks view too.
- Update downloads are smaller, and only the newest nightly test build is kept on the releases page.
- The website's module options, configuration types and endpoint list are generated from the code, and the API reference is rewritten.
- Underlying libraries are updated, including security fixes in the web framework, the test runner and the image libraries.
- Every screen follows the household's time zone. The wall, the phone, the editor and the hub all use the time zone saved in Settings, never the device or browser clock. Looking up a town fills in its time zone, and the phone says when home's day or hour is different from yours.
- A new install's 12 or 24 hour clock follows the chosen language.
- Daily weather forecasts can hide the weekday labels, the larger first day, and the Forecast heading.
- Calendar event backgrounds can be turned off on the daily and agenda views.
- The chore chart card shows one row per chore with a dot per person, so a chore five kids share is one row. Tap a dot to tick it off, press and hold to un-tick.
- Meals can be typed straight into a day without saving them to the library first.
- Settings search finds settings by their options and help text too, and each result shows which page it is on.
- The grocery list guesses the aisle for each item in every language.
- The display holds the current screen for eight seconds after a tap.
- Background and border colors have a strength slider, and the swatch shows the real color.
- The getting-started checklist has a household step, and the Family Dashboard template includes a chore chart.
- Un-ticking a finished chore on the wall takes a press and hold, like the kid tablet.
- A screen nobody has filled in yet stays out of the display rotation.
- The Back gesture on the phone closes the open sheet instead of leaving the page, and asks first if there are unsaved changes.
- Clearer wording across Settings in every language, and day and month names no longer show in English before a language finishes loading.

## Fixed

- Two phones, or a phone and the editor, changing chores, rewards, meals or routines at the same time can no longer silently drop each other's changes.
- A display held at a standing brightness ignored every tap and flick.
- On a Pi with more than one video output, the kiosk drives the output that is actually switched on.
- Weekly and every-other-week chore rotations hand over on Monday instead of Friday.
- A display could show old data when a request that started before a change finished after it.
- Sports Standings with no grouping chosen fetched one grouping and labelled it as another.
- Un-ticking a chore whose points were already spent says who is affected and by how much.
- Calendar feeds whose events carry a time but no time zone resolve in the display's time zone, so a 4 pm event no longer shows at 6 pm on a UTC hub.
- Running the installer over an existing install destroyed every uploaded picture and iCloud import. User files now survive a re-install.
- The editor password could go unchecked on some installs because the password file was read from the wrong place.
- Day-rule pictures paint under the day's events, load on a wall behind a password, and no longer wipe a rule's colour when the base background changes.
- Checking off a chore and crediting its points are saved together, so losing power in between cannot record one without the other.
- Completed to-do items are tidied after 30 days, so a list that never repeats cannot fill up.
- Adding a household member before the chore manager had loaded discarded the new name.
- Plugin fixes: installing or removing one no longer resets the panel to Browse, and two quick changes no longer leave the hub serving the previous plugin list.
- Calendar feed and day-rule fixes: a just-checked feed keeps its health badge, switching a rule from a date pattern to weekdays clears the month, and a moved or renamed picture still shows as selected.
- Both chore charts show a loading message while a freshly started Pi loads instead of "No family members yet".
- Google Calendar events with the newer colors (Radicchio, Cherry Blossom, Citron, Pistachio and custom) show their own color.
- iCloud fixes: newer Shared Album links work, and an unreachable iCloud is reported as such instead of as a bad link.
- Weather fixes: Met Office and Open-Meteo forecasts start on the right day, and switching to a source whose key is already saved no longer flashes a missing-key card.
- Todoist tasks with a time showed at the wrong time.
- Two quick taps on the same chore could undo each other.
- Date fixes: new countdown and holiday dates no longer start a day off when picked in the evening, and a time window with only a start or end reads "from 6:00 PM" or "until 9:00 AM" instead of "all day".
- Wall freshness fixes: the first layout edit no longer empties the wall's cache, a Google Calendar sign-in shows within seconds, a refused display key reloads to pick up a new one, and the phone and kids' page pick up chores and rewards another phone changed.
- The kids' page works with a password set.
- Photo fixes: videos shown from the phone fill the wall, Show on the wall sends a fitted copy, sideways photos report their upright size, and folder create and delete report the right thing.
- Chore and reward fixes: removing a person switches off a reward that was only theirs, and grocery ticks, blank ingredient rows and the "Other" aisle behave.
- Layout fixes: long all-day titles stay in their day in the calendar's schedule view, the meal planner's week view fits a landscape wall, and the photo viewer's clock shows on the wall when the editor says it is on.
- Sleep fix: with idle sleep off, the remote's Sleep no longer turns back into dimmed and lights a powered-off screen.
- Small fixes: the phone's Back gesture closes the photo viewer, a backup from a newer version is refused with a reason, arrow keys inside an editor control no longer move the module, typing a space in a screen rename no longer starts a drag, and two mislabelled options in German, French, Brazilian Portuguese and Danish are corrected.
